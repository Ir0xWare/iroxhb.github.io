gieno

gieno (short for irox & gaming enthusiast) is a Dariacore artist who's been active since 2022. (info: their Discord)

They started out making phonk but now mostly produce dariacore under a self-named style called uninstallcore. Formerly known as pvz enthusiast, they now go by gaming enthusiast. They remain somewhat active on their older alias, irox.

About

gieno (real name: Jakub) is a Polish artist, currently 13 years old (turning 14 on July 28). They began making songs in 2022 — initially phonk, but later discovered dariacore and stuck with it.
(note from gieno: thanks, beat saber <3)

At first, they released music as irox but now focus more on gaming enthusiast. They are still active on irox, but less frequently. Originally, gaming enthusiast was called pvz enthusiast, but the name was changed to reflect a broader focus beyond just Plants vs. Zombies (though they still love pvz. another note: who wants to play pvz garden warfare 2).

They create uninstallcore.

History

gieno started producing in 2022, making phonk tracks under the name irox, using samples and beats they found cool. Later, they got into dariacore and changed their style. Around early 2025, they created the new alias pvz enthusiast, focusing on video game-inspired... things i guess? Eventually, this was renamed to gaming enthusiast to avoid limiting themselves to just pvz.

uninstallcore began as a joke name but became a real thing to them. It’s not exactly a genre, more like a mindset — they just make what sounds chaotic and fun. It’s kinda just whatever.
